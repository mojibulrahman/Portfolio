01 — About

Engineering reliable vision on the factory floor

I'm a Vision Engineer based in Bangalore, India, currently supporting on-site vision engineering at Bosch Limited through Reyocto Infotech. Over the past three years I've moved through vision-focused roles at an OSS vendor for KEYENCE and at OPT Vision Limited, building and commissioning 2D/3D inspection systems for manufacturing lines — from initial camera calibration to stable production deployment.
